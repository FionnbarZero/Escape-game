# Monster Abilities Overhaul — Remaining Milestones

These are recorded design milestones only. They are not claimed as implemented by the Chef milestone.

## Milestone 2 — Molly

- Intercom Sweep with visible antenna deployment and local, crouch-safe detection zones.
- Committed Search Rush toward a recorded sound position, never continuous hidden-player tracking.
- Preserve the numbered-computer, code, hammer, camera-controller, drone, lever, and final-exit loop.

## Milestone 3 — Collector and Clockmaker

- Collector: warned possession Recall and luggage Collection Sweep, excluding ordinary inventory and secured objectives.
- Clockmaker: machinery reversal, warned stopped beats, and displayed tempo zones; no gaze-slow rule.

## Milestone 4 — Water encounters

- Drowned Guest: channel currents, warned platform reaches, and the established heavy land transition.
- Courtyard Water Creature: connected-water channel travel only, with a readable wake and no land pursuit.

## Milestone 5 — Deception encounters

- Fake Manager: forged directions, inspectable counterfeit stations, and removable authorization seals.
- False Guests: stable two-clue identification and the existing local reset reveal.

## Milestone 6 — Pursuer and Floor 2 threats

- Pursuer: supported obstacle vaults, authored barrier breaks, and physical connecting-passage interceptions.
- Ballroom Guests: Runner, Chaser, and Ambusher roles while preserving frozen tagged states and the tag-gated exit.
- Watcher: valid routing while unobserved without teleporting or watched root motion.
- Gardener: warned, gradual path growth that always leaves a route to objectives.
- Window Creature: occupied-window danger zones synchronized to switch safety.

## Global acceptance rule

Every ability must have a warning, active interval, counter, and recovery/reset. Each later milestone requires its own runtime tests and normal-input playtest attempt. Chef shockwaves and health bars are not a generic template for the roster.
