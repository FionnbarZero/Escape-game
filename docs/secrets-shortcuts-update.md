# Secrets & Shortcuts Update

## Implemented first slice: Molly Electrical Section

This slice proves the exploration loop without changing Molly's objective or detection rules.

- The reception contains two marked clue drawers and one ordinary supply drawer. Each is saved per attempt and cannot award its contents twice.
- The misfiled Room 217 key reveals the optional Forgotten Guest Suite. Its letter, covered photograph, packed luggage, and supplies are optional.
- The marked maintenance plan reveals the optional observation room. Its reinforced window gives route information, not safety or objective progress.
- A maintenance door between the main corridor and reception opens only from the corridor side. Once released, it stays open for the current Molly attempt and records a journal discovery.
- `J` now includes a persistent **Places & Discoveries** section. These records are separate from monster completion and therefore do not alter the lobby's all-journals gate.

The new rooms contain no required code, computer, lever, or final-exit condition. Molly remains active, hacked shutters still require their existing override rules, and The Noise remains disabled in this section.
